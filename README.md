## Hi there 👋, my name is Annie

🙋🏼‍♀️ About me
----------------------------------------------------
I'm from Illinois in the US, currently in the Central Standard Time Zone 🌎. I have always been something of a tech hobbyist. Early on my brothers ignited my interest starting with how to burn CDs, then how to safely utilize torrent indexes. From there I joined the 3DS modification community and began tinkering with physical modification of devices. As I got older I leaned into this love by going to college for computer engineering, falling deeply in love with the skill of electronic soldering. In my free time I can be found listening to audiobooks📚, doing logic puzzles🧠, jamming out to music🎶, playing video games🎮, or spending time with my adorable pet rats🐀
